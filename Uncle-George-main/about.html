About Uncle George

Our story, mission, and the team behind your favorite bakery.

Our Story

Founded in 2018 by students from Cavite State University.

Started as a small bakery venture, Uncle George has grown into a trusted online bread shop serving fresh, quality bread to thousands of customers across the Philippines.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To bake and deliver fresh, high-quality bread and pastries made with love and the finest ingredients.

Vision

To be the most trusted online bakery in the Philippines, recognized for excellence in taste and quality.
